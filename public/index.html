01 / How it works

Most speech apps make you build every sentence word by word. WordBridge works from fragments, the way aphasia actually works.

01/03

Tap fragments

Tap picture tiles or type a few words, misspelled is fine. "water cold" is enough.

02/03

Pick your sentence

SERV Reasoning works out what you most likely mean and offers four complete sentences.

03/03

It speaks for you

Tap once and it's spoken aloud. Save favourites to your phrasebook for next time.

02 / Two-way conversation

Listen · Understand · Reply

When someone asks you something, your answers are already there.

Listen
WordBridge hears your conversation partner through the microphone and shows the question in big, simple words.
Reply
You get five one-tap replies: yes, no, the specific choices, or "please say that again slowly."
Stay in the conversation
No more nodding along. You answer in seconds instead of minutes.

Powered by SERV Reasoning

Guessing meaning is a reasoning problem

Fragments like "water cold kitchen" can mean four different things. Every request goes through SERV Reasoning, which weighs the place, the conversation partner and your past choices before it ranks the sentences, so a small, fast model can still read you accurately. Each answer shows a short reasoning note so carers can check it.

POST /v1/chat/completions
model: gpt-5.4-mini · reasoning_effort: low

{ "fragments": "water cold",
  "context": { "place": "home" } }

→ { "sentences": [
     { "text": "Could I have a glass of
        cold water, please?",
       "tone": "request" }, … ],
    "reasoning": ["'water'+'cold' at home
       → most likely a drink request"] }
